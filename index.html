FormReady Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the FormReady Android application.

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

FormReady ("FormReady", "we", "us", or "our") is an Android application designed to help users prepare photos, signatures, documents, and other files according to application and document requirements.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use FormReady, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, FormReady may process the following categories of information.

Account Information

If you create or use a FormReady account, authentication information may be processed through Firebase Authentication.

Depending on the sign-in method, this may include:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account name or display name, where provided
  • Authentication provider information
  • Firebase-assigned user identifier
  • Authentication and security-related technical information

Firebase Authentication may also process technical information such as user-agent information and IP addresses for authentication security and abuse prevention.

2. Information You Enter in the App

You may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your preferred display name
  • Application or requirement names
  • Photo, signature, and document requirements
  • Dimensions, file-size limits, output formats, crop preferences, and related preparation settings
  • Other information that you choose to enter into FormReady

3. Photos, Signatures and Documents

FormReady provides tools for preparing photos, signatures, documents, and other files.

The core file-preparation workflows are designed to process these files locally on your Android device. FormReady does not use Firebase Authentication as a storage service for your photos, signatures, or documents.

Your files may include personal or sensitive information. You should only process files that you have the right to use and should review the requirements of the service or application to which you intend to submit them.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by FormReady may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate you securely
  • Provide sign-in and account-management functionality
  • Remember settings and preferences
  • Process and prepare files according to selected requirements
  • Save local application data such as saved requirements and history
  • Provide application features and improve reliability and security
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, and unauthorized access

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Firebase Authentication

FormReady uses Firebase Authentication, provided by Google, to support account authentication.

Depending on your selected authentication method, Firebase Authentication may process email addresses, phone numbers, authentication credentials, user profile information, user-agent information, IP addresses, and other authentication-related information.

For more information, see Google's Firebase Privacy documentation .

6. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, authentication is performed through Google's authentication services and Firebase Authentication.

Information made available by Google may be used to create or maintain your FormReady authentication profile. This may include basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile image, depending on the information provided by your Google account and the authentication flow.

Google's own privacy policies also apply to information processed directly by Google through its services.

7. Phone Authentication

If you choose phone authentication, your phone number is used for authentication and verification.

Firebase Authentication may process the phone number and related authentication and security information required to provide the service.

Standard mobile carrier or SMS charges, if applicable, may be imposed by your mobile service provider.

8. Local Storage and Application Data

FormReady may store certain application information locally on your device, including:

  • Application preferences
  • Display name
  • Language preference
  • Theme preference
  • Default output format
  • Saved requirement profiles
  • Application and preparation history
  • Other information necessary for local workflows

9. File Sharing and Export

FormReady may provide Android system functions for saving, opening, exporting, or sharing prepared files.

When you choose to share a file, the destination application or service you select receives the file according to that service's own privacy practices.

10. Data Retention

Account information associated with Firebase Authentication is retained according to the operation of the Firebase project and applicable service policies until the account is deleted or otherwise removed.

Information stored locally by FormReady remains on your device until it is removed by the app, you clear the app's data, or you uninstall the app, subject to Android device behavior and the specific type of data.

11. Account and Data Deletion Requests

If you want your FormReady account or associated personal information deleted, contact us using the support email below.

We may need to verify a request before processing it.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes.

12. Children's Privacy

FormReady is a general-purpose productivity and document- preparation application. It is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the app's functionality.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through FormReady.

Firebase Authentication provides security measures including encryption in transit and encryption at rest for supported Firebase services.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Third-Party Services

FormReady uses third-party services necessary for certain functionality, including Firebase Authentication and Google authentication services.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their processing of information may be subject to their respective policies.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FormReady's features, services, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change.

When changes are made, the Effective Date shown at the top of this policy will be updated.
